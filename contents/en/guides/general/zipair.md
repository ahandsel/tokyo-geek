## 📋 Summary

* **No frills** - No onboard entertainment; meals and drinks cost extra.
* **Food is decent** - Small portions, acceptable taste.
* **Standard seats are narrow** - Smaller than JAL or ANA.
* **Extra legroom seats are worth it** - More comfortable than JAL or ANA economy.
* **Add-ons are cheapest online, up to 3 hours before departure** - Extra luggage, meals, and carry-on allowance cost more at the airport counter.
* **Bookings cannot be changed** - The Flex Biz package, bought at booking time, is the only way to get anything back if your plans change.


## 💺 How to get seats with extra legroom

1. **Do not book a package** - Every package forces a standard seat.
2. Best: **extra legroom seats in the front section** - A small wall adds privacy and storage pockets.
3. Next best: **extra legroom seats in the center aisle**, skipping emergency exit rows.
4. On a side aisle, **avoid the window seat** - The wall curves inward and eats head space.
5. Window seats do have a **pocket that fits an iPad**.


## 📦 Packages

* **Packages are not much cheaper than buying each item separately**, and they cost you the seat choice.
* Every package is limited to standard seats, including middle seats.
* Buy the seat, meal, and baggage individually instead.
* The one package worth weighing is **Flex Biz**, covered in the cancellation section below.


## 🍱 Ordering an inflight meal

* **Order with your booking**, at least 3 hours before departure. Onboard selection is limited.
* **Size** - Small; order 2 for a long flight.
* **Taste** - Acceptable so far.
* **Timing** - You can ask to receive the meal 2 hours before arrival, for a small charge.
* **No carbonated water onboard**; only soda is sold.
* Flying from Japan? **Buy food at the 7-Eleven** near the terminal.
  * Google Maps: [7-Eleven Narita Airport Terminal 1][]
  * Narita website: [7-Eleven Terminal 1][]

## 🧳 Luggage and carry-on

* You can add luggage any time, but unused luggage is not refunded.
* **[ZIPAIR Point Club Plus][]** - Worth it if you fly ZIPAIR with checked baggage.
  * **Annual fee**: ¥5,000
  * **Benefit**: 30% off checked baggage fees
  * **Note**: Sporting goods are excluded.
  * ⚠️ **Warning**: The discount applies only on the web, after you log in as a member.


## 🛫 Airport check-in locations

Counters open **3 hours before departure and close 1 hour before**. Checked baggage must be accepted by the same 1-hour deadline.

| Airport | Check-in counter                                          | Notes                                                    |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| NRT     | Terminal 1, North Wing, 4F ([map][NRT check-in])          | The counter island varies; check the departure displays. |
| SFO     | International Terminal A, Level 3 ([map][SFO check-in])   | Closest parking is International Garage A.               |
| SJC     | Terminal B, ground floor ([map][SJC check-in])            | ⚠️ Flights depart from Terminal A.                        |

Mobile check-in is available on the Narita-San Francisco and Narita-San Jose routes.


## ⚠️ Changing or cancelling a booking

* **You cannot change a booking** - ZIPAIR does not allow customer-initiated date or flight changes after purchase.
* **Flex Biz is the only flexibility** - Add it when you make the original booking. It cannot be added later, and it is not sold on Seoul routes.
* **How it works** - Cancel the booking, receive the eligible amount as a ZIPAIR voucher, book the new flight separately, and pay with the voucher. ZIPAIR never moves the original ticket.

| Cancel before departure | Refunded as a voucher                      |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 30 days or more         | Fare, taxes, and eligible service fees     |
| 7 to 29 days            | 80% of the fare, taxes, and eligible fees  |
| 0 to 6 days             | Applicable taxes only, and no voucher      |

* The clock runs from the **scheduled departure time**, not midnight, so 7 days means 168 hours.
* A **3% issuance fee** is taken from the voucher amount.
* The voucher is valid for **180 days**, is issued in the currency of the original booking, and cannot be transferred to anyone else.
* Flex Biz also adds advance seat selection and **8 kg of extra carry-on allowance**, for 15 kg total. See the [Service Package - ZIPAIR Official Website][].
* **Flights to and from the USA and Canada** - Cancel within 24 hours of booking, with departure at least 7 days away, and the full amount goes back to your original payment method. No Flex Biz needed.
* **If ZIPAIR cancels or delays the flight**, its disruption policy applies instead, with its own refund and rebooking options.
* [Refunds via Voucher - ZIPAIR Official Website][]
* [Fare Rules - ZIPAIR Official Website][]


## ✈️ Travel tips

* **Bring a tablet, not a laptop** - Seats are too narrow for a laptop and have a stand for a tablet or phone.
* **Charging** - USB-A ports work well; the in-seat power plugs sit at an awkward angle and fall out.
* **Bring snacks and drinks**.
* **Seats 19D and 19E are not separated by a hard border**.


## 📶 Onboard Wi-Fi

* Free but **very slow**. Messaging apps work; browsing does not.
